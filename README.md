# About Me

Hi, I'm **Amin Farahani Fard**.

I am a B.Sc. Electrical Engineering student specializing in **Control Systems**
at the Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). My work combines
control-system modeling, optimization, embedded systems, machine learning, and
computer vision.

I am currently conducting research on **Data-Enabled Predictive Control
(DeePC)** as a remote research intern at the AI2S Laboratory, Istanbul Technical
University. I am also interested in model predictive control, learning-based
control, cyber-physical systems, intelligent robotics, and AI applications in
electrical engineering.

Alongside research, I have served as a teaching assistant across control,
signals and systems, electronics, programming, and mathematics courses. I enjoy
turning theoretical control concepts into reproducible simulations and working
embedded prototypes.

## Research Interests

`Model Predictive Control` · `Data-Driven Control` · `Learning-Based Control` ·
`Intelligent Control` · `Embedded Systems` · `Computer Vision` ·
`Smart Grid Monitoring`

## Featured Projects

- [**Multirate MPC Paper Reproduction**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/mpc-inverted-pendulum-paper-reproduction) -
  MATLAB reproduction of constrained multirate LQ/MPC control for a two-wheel
  inverted-pendulum robot.
- [**STM32F103 Interactive Control System**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/stm32f103-interactive-control-system) -
  LCD menu, ADC, interrupts, timers, UART, LEDs, and MPU6050 integration.
- [**Ball and Beam Control System**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/ball-and-beam-control-system) -
  modeling and closed-loop control in MATLAB and Simulink.
- [**Inverted Pendulum Modeling and Control**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/inverted-pendulum-modeling-control) -
  Lagrangian modeling, system identification, and controller comparison.
- [**Custom YOLO Object Detection**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/yolo-custom-object-detection) -
  custom-dataset training, evaluation, and image/video inference.
- [**NTC Wheatstone-Bridge Sensor Fusion**](https://github.com/amin-farahani-fard/ntc-wb-fusion) -
  embedded sensing, MPU6050 fusion, and a real-time monitoring dashboard.
